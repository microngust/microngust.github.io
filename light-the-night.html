Latest Release

Shadows & Light

Released: March 15, 2025

Our most ambitious album to date, "Shadows & Light" explores the duality of human existence through thundering riffs, soaring melodies, and lyrics that delve into both darkness and hope. Produced by legendary rock producer Alex Wharton, this 12-track journey represents our musical evolution while staying true to our raw, energetic sound.
